Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ooga Labs handles information when you use our Discord applications.

Information We Store

The Server Calendar application stores information needed to provide its calendar functionality, such as event names, dates, descriptions, Discord server IDs, and Discord user IDs associated with events.

How We Use Information

This information is used only to provide and maintain the application's features.

Data Sharing

Ooga Labs does not sell or share stored application data with third parties, except where required by law.

Data Removal

Server administrators may request removal of data associated with their server.

Contact

For privacy questions or data removal requests, please contact the developer through Discord.
